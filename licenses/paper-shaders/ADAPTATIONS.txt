Moiré adaptations, 2026-10-29:
- Paper Texture and Water use the editor's mainImage entry point and image/UV bindings.
- Water animation uses the editor timeline; colors use the editor's controls.
- Paper's randomizer input uses the editor's generated noise texture.
- Output is converted to straight alpha for the editor's filter compositing path.

The original LICENSE and NOTICE are included unchanged as LICENSE.txt and NOTICE.txt.
These adaptations are not endorsed by Paper and do not grant rights to other services' code.

2026-10-29 input profile update: Moire Paper uses a newly authored deterministic
xorshift32 RGB noise input, seed 20261029, instead of the legacy texture helper.
Its Three.js texture adapter and LUT helpers are documented separately in
docs/rights-transition/INPUTS.md. This can change the paper texture appearance.
Upstream filter code, copyright, LICENSE and NOTICE are retained.

2026-10-29 composition profile update: Moire Paper and Water now use the newly
authored moire-compositor-v1 wrapper for new effects only. It uses fragment
coordinates, premultiplied filter-result interpolation, W3C mathematical blend
definitions, canvas transforms and masks. This may change sampling or edge
pixels relative to the unpublished stage03 prototype. The adapted filter kernels
and original LICENSE/NOTICE remain unchanged. Details: docs/rights-transition/COMPOSITOR.md.

2026-10-29/30 printing and refraction integration:
- Image Dithering and Fluted Glass are newly imported from the same pinned
  official commit, with original source hashes in SOURCE_PRINT_GLASS.json.
- Adapted entry point, image/UV/resolution/color bindings and editor transforms.
- Dither has source-pixel cell sizing, editor UV orientation and original input alpha.
- Glass uses straight-alpha output, premultiplied Amount interpolation and
  source-pixel blur sizing. Keep input matte clips alpha by default; Refracted
  edges retains the original material/edge behavior. Amount zero is a bypass.
- The package NOTICE has an additional copyright line. Its complete original
  text is included as PACKAGE-NOTICE.txt, alongside the unchanged root NOTICE.txt
  and LICENSE.txt. This also preserves package notice coverage for Paper/Water.
Details and scope: docs/rights-transition/PRINT_GLASS.md.
